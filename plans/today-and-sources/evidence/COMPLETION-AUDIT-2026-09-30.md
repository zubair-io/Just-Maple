The active goal builds missing [Notes, Today and Sources requirements](../../../docs/product/PRD-TODAY-AND-SOURCES.md) first, then the [main PRD](../../../docs/product/PRD-JUST-MAPLE.md). ## Delivery sequence

1. Local-first state foundation: atomic signal-backed managed sessions, shared latest-draft queue, owner-token handoff, receipt preservation and terminal operation recovery. Verified and built; see [design/evidence](../../../docs/engineering/NOTE-STATE-AND-SYNC.md).
2. Missing note workflows: linked-task details and safe completion undo; authorized recording playback and captured text; durable source-search pagination with explicit insertion; separate phone file/processing freshness. Integrated automated verification passed; see results below.
3. Quality capture infrastructure is now implemented: durable pre-dispatch provider evidence and an explicit Mac capture of the accepted UI projection plus matching frozen database. See [capture contracts](../../../docs/engineering/QUALITY-CAPTURE.md). Production callsites and manifest-bound offline export are now covered in the subsequent delivery below. Remaining product validation includes independent human adjudication, accessibility and real-device sync. Exhaustive upstream lineage and historical coverage remain unproven under the stricter capture contract; they are not prerequisites to reporting observed final-output recall or top-ten usefulness with honest scope and denominators. The scorer leaves its proposed gate fields unassessed with unknown coverage, but still reports observed metrics. Capture/export infrastructure alone establishes neither model quality nor release readiness.
