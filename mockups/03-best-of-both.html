Launch Strategy: Mercury Maple

The vision for Mercury Maple is to create a unified workspace that combines the mystique of Mercury with the clarity of minimal design. Every interaction feels intentional, every surface purposeful.

"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication." — Leonardo da Vinci

Key pillars of our approach:

  • Dark atmosphere without gloss or blur
  • Clean typography hierarchy
  • Intentional color use (maple accent only where meaningful)
  • Generous spacing and breathing room

See also: [[Design System]], [[Color Palette]], [[Typography]]
